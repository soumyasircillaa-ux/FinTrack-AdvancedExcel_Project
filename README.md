## 📌 Project Overview 

This project focuses on analyzing FinTrack sales performance across multiple countries, products, customer segments, and discount bands to understand key profit drivers.
The objective is to identify growth opportunities, improve pricing and discount strategies, and increase the overall profit margin from 14.2% to 18%.
The analysis provides actionable insights through dashboards and reports to support better business decisions and sustainable growth


------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🛠 Tools Used

1. Microsoft Excel
2. Pivot Tables
3. Charts (Bar, Pie, Line, Donut)
4. Slicers
5. Looker Studio (Dashboard and Reports)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


## 📈 Key KPIs

* Total Sales: $118.7M
* Total Profit: $1.69M
* Discount : $2.2M
* Profit Margin: 14.2%

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


## Project Workflow
1. Data Collection: Imported raw FinTrack data into Excel.
2. Data Cleaning: Removed duplicates, fixed data types, and standardized product names.
3. Created calculated columns: Sales, Profit, Discounts, Profit Margins, Year and Month
4. Pivot Analysis: Created summaries for sales, quantity, rating, and customer types.
5. Dashboard Design: Built a visual dashboard with filters and dynamic charts.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 💡 Insights

1: United States generates the highest sales among all countries.
2: Paseo product is the most profitable product.
3: High discounts reduce profit margins.
4: Sales show seasonal patterns across months.
5: Government segment contributes the highest revenue.
6: Products with higher manufacturing costs reduce profit margins.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## 🚀 Recommendations
1. Focus on North America market
2. Increase Paseo promotions
3. Reduce high discounts
4. Expand government segment strategy
